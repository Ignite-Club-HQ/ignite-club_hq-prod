- Club chat reads and writes check the original human through club_domain.read_has_club_membership in addition to local participants; protected legacy reads use updates, real DMs and competition audiences keep separate scope, and scheduled/replayed/forwarded sends reuse sendAs. Why: stored participants and mirrored roles must not retain removed-club access; metadata cannot convert a club chat into an exempt scope.
- open_conversation_query is the composite-query twin of open_conversation for first-page paint: same legacy-query denial, removal fence, private-name window, club membership via club_domain's is_club_member query, and message/pin visibility; every refusal returns Err/traps so clients retry the consensus update. Why: first messages appear ~1s sooner without widening access; protected modes stay update-only.
- Standard staff chats (Coaches/Team Admins/Club Committee) are created, adopted and repaired only after requireClubAdminLive; a live club admin may top up a bound standard chat without being a member, but personal/private group management stays unchanged. Member caps count the distinct resulting audience, and canonical ensure_conversation refuses an existing ID with a different club/team scope. Why: ordinary members must not claim staff-chat ownership, retries must not trip the cap, and ID collisions must not merge scopes.
