- Photo reaction queries use account plus sorted unique loaded photo IDs; same-account carry-forward data is filtered to those IDs. Mutations cancel all account reaction reads before optimism and settlement, overlay pending changes on new-list reads, and roll back only their own photo/user pair. Why: pagination, realtime/resume and account transitions must not hide saved reactions or overwrite pending changes.
- Album reaction controls represent the visible photo ID, not the album or feed cover; saved rows are never reassigned. Why: upload-order slides and descending-order covers can differ.
